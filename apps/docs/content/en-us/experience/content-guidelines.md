## Action labels

Describe the outcome with a clear verb, such as “Save changes” or “Add member”. Short labels work when the surrounding context is sufficient. Avoid ambiguous “Yes” and “No” actions.

## Forms and errors

Labels describe the input; placeholders provide examples and do not replace labels. Validation explains what happened and how to fix it while preserving valid user input.

## Empty and loading states

Distinguish first use, no search results, insufficient access and failed requests. Loading text describes the operation; failure states offer meaningful recovery.

## Consistency and localization

Use the same term for the same concept. Use sentence case in English and avoid constructing translatable sentences by concatenation. Allow room for different language lengths.
